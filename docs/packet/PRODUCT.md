# AS Sales App

The sales rep's entire funnel in one app stacked on GoHighLevel: leads validated, research briefed, decks generated, proposals built from call transcripts, invoices drafted, with the rep never touching the CRM. GHL stays the pipeline backend and system of record; the app is where humans decide, and every decision writes back through one visible, logged seam.

**Who it is for**

| Persona | Access |
|---|---|
| admin (Zachery, Mitchell) | Everything: all leads, admin cluster, invoice approval |
| operator (sales rep; 1 at launch, built for 3 to 4) | Own leads, research, decks, proposals, meetings; invoices and recordings read-only |

**Core features:** lead list + Claude validation with a human suspect queue · auto-research briefs · one-click Presenton decks (Discovery Call, Follow Up, Strategy Session) with the booking calendar on the last slide · GHL stage mirror with silence paths · Smart Pricing Table proposals fed by Fathom transcripts, tweaked in natural language · Invoice Ninja two-invoice drafts with in-app approval.

**Non-goals (MVP):** full in-app invoice management · GHL custom-field deck push · multi-rep routing admin · management reporting depth · post-Lead-Won workflows · any customer-facing surface.
